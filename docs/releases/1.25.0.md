A vault for files, your next meeting, live downloads, focus breaks and more.

## New

- **Vault** — drag files onto the notch to hold them on the way somewhere, then drag them out of the notch into Finder, Mail, a chat or a browser's upload box to copy them; hold **⌘** while dragging to move instead. Drag them out one at a time or all at once, share them (AirDrop, Messages, Mail), zip them into one archive, or have the vault clear itself after an hour, a day or a week. Text, links and images dragged out of a page work too. The vault points to your files rather than copying them, so it costs no disk space; things from temporary places (Mail attachments, screenshots, a browser's images) get a copy of their own. **Settings → Deck → Vault**.
- **Next meeting** — a ring that fills as your next meeting approaches, from every calendar in the Calendar app. Its card has the time, place and a **Join** button for Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Webex and FaceTime links, plus the rest of today. The notch opens a few minutes before (1, 5 or 10 — your pick) with Join right there. macOS asks once for access to your calendars, when you click **Allow Calendar Access**.
- **Live status** — downloads from Safari and Chrome, and AirDrop, get a cell of their own while they run, and the notch opens as each one lands with **Open**, **Show in Finder** and **Add to Vault**. Plugging in or unplugging shows the battery charging, with time to full or time left.
- **Focus breaks** — turn on breaks and the timer runs focus, a short break, focus again, and a long break every few sessions, each starting by itself if you like. A **Skip** button, the phase in the card, and today's focused time with the last seven days as bars. Pick a Shortcut to run as focus starts and stops to switch Do Not Disturb.
- **Clipboard search and snippets** — search your clips (click the field, type, Return copies the first match), star a clip to keep it as a snippet, and manage snippets in Settings. The **Aa** button turns rich text on the clipboard into plain text, and a setting does that for every copy.
- **Volume and output** — the Now Playing card has the system volume, mute, and a menu to switch between speakers, headphones, AirPods and displays.

## Install

Already on 1.24? NotchDeck offers this update in the notch — choose **Update** — or press **Settings → General → Check for Updates**.

- **macOS 15 or later** — `brew install --cask mr-who-root/notchdeck/notchdeck`, or download `NotchDeck.dmg` and drag NotchDeck to Applications. The build is ad-hoc signed, not notarized: if macOS says it cannot verify the app, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**, or run

  ```sh
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/NotchDeck.app
  ```

  After each update macOS asks again for calendar access, because the app's signature changes with every ad-hoc build.

- **Windows 10/11** — download `NotchDeck-Setup.exe` and run it. It is not code-signed, so SmartScreen asks first: **More info → Run anyway**.
